The result view is a print sheet. This tutorial lays it out for a report and exports it.

Start a new project with **File › New** and click **▶ Run**.

## 1. The paper

Click an empty part of the sheet to select the paper. The **Properties** section of the sidebar shows its settings:

<Figure src="/media/tutorials/reporting/01-paper.png" alt="The result view with the paper selected and its properties in the sidebar" caption="The paper selected: extents, ticks, paper size and orientation in the sidebar." />

- **Left X**, **Right X**, **Bottom Y**, **Top Y**: the part of the model on the sheet. **Use model bounds** fits the whole model.
- **Paper size** and **Paper orientation**.

The same settings are in **Results › Display › Settings**:

<Figure src="/media/tutorials/reporting/03-view-settings.png" alt="The View Settings window" caption="View Settings: paper, grid and ticks, and plot extents." />

## 2. Annotations

The example model's sheet already has a title, two lines of text with the published and calculated factors of safety, and a material table. Add more from **Results › Annotations**: text, a material table, a plot of slice data, or a colour bar.

Show all surfaces (**Results › Display**, surfaces list, **All surfaces**) and add a **colour bar** to explain the colours:

<Figure src="/media/tutorials/reporting/02-color-bar.png" alt="All surfaces coloured by factor of safety, with a colour bar" caption="All surfaces with a colour bar." />

Drag annotations to place them. Select one to edit its text, font, size and colour in the sidebar. A text can show values from the model: `#Title`, `#Client`, `#Revision`, `#Method`, `#FOS` and others, and any parameter as `#name`. See [Reporting](/docs/reporting#text-fields) for the list.

## 3. Reuse the layout

**Results › Templates › Export template** saves the layout (annotations, display options, paper and extents) as a file. **Import template** applies it to another model, so every model in a report gets the same title block.

## 4. Export

**Results › Export › PDF** saves the sheet as a vector PDF. The menu next to it has the other formats:

<Figure src="/media/tutorials/reporting/04-export.png" alt="The export menu with PDF, PNG, Excel and CSV options" caption="Export: the sheet as PDF or PNG, the slices of the critical surface as Excel or CSV." />

The slice table lists every slice of the critical surface with its geometry, weight, strength, pore pressure and forces, ready for a calculation appendix.
