After an analysis the canvas switches to **result mode**. It shows a print sheet with the model, the critical slip surface and the annotations. Switch back with **Edit** on the floating toolbar; **Results** returns to the result view as long as the result is valid.

## The summary

The **Results** sidebar on the right (collapse or open it at its header row, see [The cSlope window](/docs/interface#layout)) has three parts: **Summary**, **Layers** (see [Reporting](/docs/reporting#layers)) and the settings of the selected object, named after it (*Sheet*, *Text*, *Material table*, …). The summary shows:

- **Min FOS**: the lowest factor of safety found, colored red below 1.0, amber below 1.5 and green above. With partial factors it reads *Min FOS (design values)* and has no amber: 1.0 and above is green.
- **Method** used.
- **Surfaces**: how many surfaces were analyzed and how many of them have a factor of safety below 1.0.
- **Elapsed**: the time the analysis took.
- With both directions analyzed: the **sliding direction** of the critical surface and the factor of safety of the **other direction**.
- With [custom surfaces](/docs/search#custom-surfaces): a row **Custom surface 1**, **2**, … for each, with its factor of safety (*1.234*) or *no solution* and the reason, for example a circle that misses the ground, a surface that leaves the model, is too shallow or passes through impenetrable material, or one that does not converge. The circles are numbered first, then the polylines. Hover over the name to see the circle's center and radius or the number of points. The PDF summary lists them too.
- Warnings, for example surfaces discarded because they did not reach equilibrium.

The status bar also shows the factor of safety and the method.

### Notes

The notes are folded into one line under the summary (*3 notes*); click it to show one line per note. The **?** next to a note opens this section.

- **Surfaces discarded because they did not reach equilibrium**: for these trial surfaces the method found no factor of safety that satisfies equilibrium within the iteration limits, so they are left out of the minimum. A few are normal; many can point to a search range that reaches unrealistic surfaces.
- **Surfaces smaller than the minimum slip area or depth were skipped**: the minimum slip area and minimum slip depth set in the analysis options leave small surfaces out of the search. A small local failure, such as a low cut at the toe, can be among the skipped surfaces. Set both to 0 (no limit, the default) to include them.

## Display options

**Results › Display** controls what the sheet shows. The options can be set before a run; they take effect when there is a result.

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Critical only** / **All surfaces** / **Filter by FOS** | Show the critical surface, every analyzed surface, or only surfaces up to a factor of safety (*FOS ≤*, default 1.5) |
| **Custom surfaces** | In the *Surfaces* menu, with custom surfaces: each one dashed, with its own factor of safety label (off by default) |
| **Show slices** | The slices of the critical surface |
| **Show FOS label** | The factor of safety next to the surface |
| **Show center mark** | The center of the critical circle |
| **Soil colors** | The material colors |
| **Plastic zone** / **FE mesh** | With a finite element result: the plastic zone (on by default) and the final mesh (off by default) |

Each option turned on is a row in the sidebar's **Layers** list, where its order among the other layers and the annotations is set ([Layers](/docs/reporting#layers)). The grid is set in the sidebar's **Sheet** section (see [Reporting](/docs/reporting#paper-and-extents)). The search limits are drawn under the result as in the edit view, unless only custom surfaces were analyzed.

With **All surfaces**, each surface is colored by its factor of safety, from red (lowest) through yellow and green to blue. Add a **color bar** annotation to show the scale (see [Reporting](/docs/reporting)).

## Results plots

**Results › Plots › Results plot** opens a window with plots of the analyzed surfaces:

- **Slip surface variables**: a quantity along a slip surface, against the slice number or x. The quantities are shear strength, friction angle, cohesion, base cohesion, pore pressure, pore water force, slice weight, cohesion strength, resisting force, pulling force and the effective normal force N′ on the base (negative on a slice the summary's notes flag). Hovering a point marks that slice on the result canvas. Choose the surface in **Slip surface**: the critical one or any other analyzed surface, listed with its factor of safety F ("Critical – F 1.250").
- **F against λ** (Morgenstern–Price only): the factors of safety from moment (Fm) and force (Ff) equilibrium against λ. F is where they agree within the tolerance.

**Export CSV** saves the plot's values: every quantity per slice of the surface shown, or λ, Fm and Ff. The ▾ beside it chooses a decimal point (1.5) or a decimal comma (1,5, with `;` as the separator, for spreadsheets in many European locales); the choice is remembered for the next export, also in the Sensitivity and Probabilistic windows.

## Slice data

The slices of the critical surface can be exported as a table: **Results › Export › ▾ › Slice data (Excel)…** or **(CSV)…**. The table lists, for every slice, its position, width, height, area, base angle and length, weight, surface load, base material, c, φ, pore pressure, effective normal force and shear strength, with a header that identifies the model, method, factor of safety and surface. See [Reporting](/docs/reporting#export).

## When the result is cleared

Any change to the model that affects the analysis clears its result: run the analysis again to see the new one. Changing the display options or the annotations does not clear the result.
