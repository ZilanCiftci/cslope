# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information (including client data) are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through the contact details at [ibirico.se](https://ibirico.se). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
